### Overview
- SnapTrace creates a daily highlight movie (SnapTrace Movie) from your photos and short videos, helping you preserve memories from trips, events, and special moments.
- Photos capture a single moment, but they cannot always express the atmosphere and emotions of the scene. SnapTrace automatically records the moments before each photo without requiring you to manually start video recording, creating a movie that brings your memories back to life.
- SnapTrace is ideal for sharing travel and event memories with family and friends who live far away.

### Features
- When you launch the app, buffering of Snap videos (short videos used to create SnapTrace Movies) starts automatically.
- When you take a photo, the previous up to 4 seconds of video are saved as a Snap video.
- Normal video recording is also available.
- You can take photos and create Snap videos even while normal video recording is in progress.
- Press the "Create SnapTrace Movie" button to combine all Snap videos from the day into one SnapTrace Movie.
- Both normal videos and SnapTrace Movies are saved as MP4 files in Full HD (1920×1080) at 30fps.
  - (SnapTrace is designed for preserving atmosphere and memories, rather than creating professional video productions.)
- Snap videos captured in portrait orientation are placed in the center of a landscape 1920×1080 video with black bars on both sides. This format is optimized for viewing SnapTrace Movies on widescreen displays such as TVs.

### SnapTrace Movie Editing

Long press the "Create SnapTrace Movie" button to enter the editing screen.

- Only SnapTrace Movies created on the same device with the same app version can be edited.
Editing steps

1. Select the SnapTrace Movie file you want to edit.
2. The movie will be played in the Preview area.
3. Press the "Go to edit mode" button to switch to editing mode.
4. Each Snap video is played in a loop. Press the "Cut" button on Snap videos you want to remove.
5. Press the "Go to preview mode" button to return to preview mode.
6. Press the "Save" button to save the edited SnapTrace Movie.
      - The edited movie is saved with the same file name. The original movie file is kept with a renamed file name.

### Notes

- Snap videos are not displayed in standard photo viewer apps such as Photos or Gallery.
- During normal video recording, rotating the device between portrait and landscape does not change the orientation of the recorded Snap video. Photos are captured according to the device orientation.
- When switching between the front and rear cameras, or when changing device orientation, SnapTrace restarts video buffering.
- The time required to create a SnapTrace Movie depends on the total length of the movie.
