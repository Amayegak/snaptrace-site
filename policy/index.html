SnapTrace Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

SnapTrace is a video recording and media creation application. This application respects user privacy and does not collect personal information.

Permissions Used

Camera Permission

SnapTrace uses the device camera to record videos and capture images.

Microphone Permission

SnapTrace may use the device microphone when recording videos with audio.

Media and Storage Access

SnapTrace accesses media files stored on the device in order to:

  • Save recorded videos
  • Generate video content from local media

Data Collection

SnapTrace does not collect, transmit, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

Local Storage

Videos and media created using SnapTrace are stored locally on the user's device. The developer does not have access to user files or media content.

Third-Party Services

SnapTrace does not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

Children's Privacy

SnapTrace does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated in the future. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the app store listing.
